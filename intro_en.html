FSSplicer

No more squinting at raw spreadsheet numbers.

This is the entry point to "horse data editing"

Full Stride has no in-game screen for editing horse stats. Every horse's name and abilities are managed in an external spreadsheet and imported into the game. Even just the visual parameters — tack and coat color — span over 50 columns. Finding the one horse you want, locating the right column, guessing what a value means, and typing it in by hand: even finishing a single horse takes real concentration if you're not used to it. If you mistake one column for another, you have no way of noticing. Try scrolling sideways below.

Let's say you just want to fix mane_color_2 (mane color 2) for horse ID H1024. Can you find it?

You just want to fix one field. Yet before you get there, you're counting columns, counting rows.

↓

What if you could just look at the field name and change the value?

Here's how FSSplicer changes that

With FSSplicer, you just adjust values in your browser while looking at field names and current values. Once you confirm, a CSV is generated with only that one horse's row replaced. You never need to worry about column position, and there's no risk of touching another horse's data. Just paste the generated CSV into the matching row.

What changes

"This horse should have had a bit more stamina," "this horse should be stronger overall, even if the start is terrible" — everyone has their own read on a racehorse. FSSplicer lets you turn that read into numbers, easily. That's FSSplicer.
